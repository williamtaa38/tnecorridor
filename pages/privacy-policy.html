We may collect personal data:

We may process personal data to:

  • create and administer student accounts
  • respond to enquiries
  • understand academic background and study goals
  • recommend suitable programmes and pathways
  • conduct education and readiness assessments
  • provide university and programme information
  • support applications and document preparation

  • coordinate C-25 and student pooling activities
  • assess scholarship interest or potential eligibility
  • arrange counselling, events and appointments
  • coordinate accommodation, arrival or student services
  • verify identity and submitted information
  • prevent fraud and misuse
  • maintain Website security
  • improve our services and user experience
  • conduct internal analysis and reporting
  • comply with legal and regulatory obligations

  • send service-related communications; and
  • send marketing communications where permitted.

We will not use personal data for a materially different purpose without providing appropriate notice or obtaining consent where required.

5. Required and Optional Information

Certain information may be required to:

  • create an account
  • assess programme suitability
  • process an enquiry
  • support an application
  • verify academic eligibility; or
  • communicate with a university.

Optional information may help us provide more personalised recommendations but is not compulsory unless clearly identified otherwise.

6. Consent and Withdrawal

Where consent is required, you may withdraw that consent by contacting us.

Withdrawal will not affect processing already lawfully carried out. It may, however, affect our ability to continue providing services that depend on the relevant information.

You may unsubscribe from promotional emails by using the unsubscribe function provided or by contacting us.

Service communications relating to an active account, enquiry or application may continue where reasonably necessary.

7. Automated and AI-Assisted Recommendations

We may use automated tools or artificial intelligence to assist with:

  • programme matching
  • pathway recommendations
  • academic-readiness assessments
  • scholarship matching
  • enquiry classification
  • student-support functions; and
  • service improvement.

Such tools are intended to support, rather than replace, appropriate human review.

Automated recommendations do not constitute a university admission decision, scholarship decision, visa decision or professional advice.

Users should verify important recommendations with the relevant university, institution or qualified adviser.

Where an automated recommendation may materially affect a user, we will seek to provide appropriate information about its purpose and allow human review or clarification where reasonably available.

Where personal data is transferred internationally, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that the transfer is permitted and that appropriate safeguards are applied, having regard to applicable law.

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal data against:

  • unauthorised access
  • accidental loss
  • misuse
  • alteration
  • disclosure; and
  • destruction.

Measures may include access controls, authentication, encryption, secure hosting, monitoring, backups and staff confidentiality requirements.

However, no internet transmission or electronic-storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should protect their passwords and notify us promptly if they suspect unauthorised account activity.

13. Data Protection Officer and Data Breaches

Where required by applicable law or regulatory guidance, TA Alliance will appoint and register one or more data protection officers responsible for supporting compliance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 and related requirements.

If we have reason to believe that a personal-data breach has occurred, we will assess, contain, investigate and remediate the incident as soon as practicable.

Where notification is required, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner in the required manner and generally within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of the breach. If the breach causes or is likely to cause significant harm, we will notify affected data subjects without unnecessary delay and, where applicable, within seven (7) days after notifying the Commissioner.

We will maintain appropriate breach records and cooperate with lawful regulatory enquiries.
